Llamatek

Llamatek provides stylized low poly vehicle packs built for real game development. Expect realistic proportions, full interiors, modular variations, working parts, and consistent setup across every package.

Includes more vehicles than strictly necessary. ❤️

Build it your way

Every vehicle is built from individual parts, giving you the freedom to mix, match, swap, and customize. Doors, bumpers, lights, wheels, interiors and body panels. Build the vehicle you want, or take the parts further and create something entirely your own.

One vehicle. Many possibilities.
